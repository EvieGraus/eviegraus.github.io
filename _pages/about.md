## **Hello!**

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Luxembourg. My research interests are in Development Economics and Economic Geography.

In my dissertation, I study how road infrastructure shapes sustainable and inclusive development in Sub-Saharan Africa. My current projects examine the effects of roads on child health, regional development, and the allocation of employment across sectors.

Methodologically, I combine geospatial data, large-scale household surveys, and causal econometric methods to study how changes in connectivity and market access affect local economic and social outcomes.
